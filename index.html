End-to-End B2B Lead Generation & GTM Automation

One system that takes a lead from first search to booked conversation, with every step logged.

  • Finds prospects: runs weekdays at 8 AM, pulls ICP-matching US decision makers from Apollo, then cleans and de-duplicates them.
  • Enriches: queues each lead in Clay and flags any enrichment that hasn't come back within 24 hours.
  • Validates: checks every business email with MillionVerifier and logs incomplete or risky leads.
  • Enrolls: checks HubSpot and eligibility rules, adds safe leads to Instantly and updates the HubSpot contact.
  • Triages replies: OpenAI classifies each reply, HubSpot is updated, Slack alerts sales to hot leads and unsubscribes go on the blocklist.
